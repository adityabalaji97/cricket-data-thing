Speed

Four seconds down to one call

Measured on production with Marco Jansen's link. The page now waits on a 162 KB player list, then a fixed half-second pause, then five stats calls that must all finish, including a batting dismissals call (1.6–1.8 seconds) that the bowling screen doesn't use. The verdict screen needs one small summary, cached at the edge and warmed for upcoming fixtures.

For sign-off

Decisions before any code

  1. Peer pool

    Strong opposition only. Against every side, the cheapest ODI bowlers since 2023 are from Namibia, Oman, Nepal and the USA, so the strips would measure everyone against associate cricket. Against top-12 sides, Jansen sits among the 114 bowlers he actually competes with. Proposed: major leagues + top-12 T20Is; ODIs and Tests v top 12.

  2. Season metric

    Wins added splits cleanly into bat and ball and answers "did he win games". Bowling Impact is too noisy to rate bowlers on, so it moves below the fold. Proposed: wins added on the first screen.

  3. Form scope

    Form follows the scope, so Jansen's Hundred games (no par data) drop out and his last chip is 11 July. The other way shows all recent games, with neutral chips where par is missing. Proposed: all recent games, neutral where needed.

  4. Verdict line

    Rules, not an LLM. The strongest rank becomes the claim, and a season swing of more than one win, either way, becomes the second sentence. Two lines at most, in your vocabulary. Proposed: rules, with a review list of 50 players before launch.

  5. Share link

    A preview image of this screen at /img/player/<id>.png, so pasting the link in WhatsApp or X shows the verdict without a screenshot. Proposed: build with the summary endpoint.

  6. Not Weston's card

    No single SD rating, no "translated" figures, no big ± hero number. Our picture is the spread of every peer with the player marked, and the season trend split into bat and ball. Proposed: as shown.
