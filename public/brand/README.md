# Hindsight brand images

Instagram profile pictures, 1080×1080 PNG (Instagram crops to a circle and shows it at about 110px).

The mark (chosen direction, Oct 2026): a lowercase h whose stem is a cricket bat, with the red ball as its full stop.
`hindsight-mark.svg` is the vector original.

- `instagram-avatar-bat-h.png`: the mark alone
- `instagram-avatar-bat-h-wordmark.png`: the mark with "hindsight" spelled out underneath (readable at 110px; at very
  small sizes the mark alone holds up better)

Earlier options:

- `instagram-avatar-a-lime-h.png`: lime H on the brand near-black (#0a0c11 / #b6f24a)
- `instagram-avatar-b-h-ball.png`: the same H with the site logo's red ball (#e5484d) as its full stop
- `instagram-avatar-c-lime-disc.png`: inverted, a dark H on lime (the brightest in a feed)

Drawn with the share images' own fonts (Barlow Semi Condensed Bold, api/_lib/fonts) so they match the graphics.
